========================================================================
OFFICIAL FUNOBOTZ 3D CHARACTER ASSET INSERTION FOLDER
========================================================================

Place official Funobotz hackathon 3D models (.glb / .gltf) in this directory:

1. Guide Character:
   File: public/models/funobotz_guide.glb  (or funobotz_mascot.glb)
   Location in world: Village Entrance / Spawn area (X: 2.8, Z: 17.5)
   Role: Introduces the "Emergency Route Rescue" mission.

2. Engineering Character:
   File: public/models/funobotz_engineer.glb
   Location in world: Bridge Construction Site (X: -4.2, Z: 6.5)
   Role: Practical bridge repair and alignment guidance.

3. Hospital Medic Character:
   File: public/models/funobotz_medic.glb
   Location in world: Hospital Emergency Bay (X: 4.5, Z: -26.0)
   Role: Reacts to route clearance and celebrates ambulance arrival.

When official models are placed here, the game automatically detects, loads,
and swaps them in, preserving all in-world collision, floating dialogue tags,
and child-friendly interaction mechanics.
